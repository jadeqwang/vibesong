Half one played like The Lost Vikings: three heroes, one verb each, and no room you could clear alone. In half two, reality levels up and so does the genre. The 3D world plays as a JRPG with no FIGHT command. You win a battle by fixing the enemy until it wants to join you.

Look

As dense as the keyframes, or denser

The concept keyframes' heroes are about 64–70 voxels tall, with heads about a fifth of their height. Ours are carved from pixel art, which makes them chibi and turns the painted shading into voxel noise. The plan is to rebuild them as 3D models in code at about 96 voxels tall. The bugs and their features get built as matched pairs, so a conversion flies each voxel of the bug into its place in the feature.
