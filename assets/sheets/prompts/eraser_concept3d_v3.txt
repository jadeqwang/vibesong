Cinematic 3D concept render sheet for an animated music video boss, "THE ERASER", based on the attached painted concept (same character design: giant rectangular school eraser with a cardboard sleeve band, sleepy-grumpy face). Stylised feature-animation 3D (Pixar-like appeal), soft matte rubber with subsurface scattering, golden-hour sun with long shadows, atmospheric haze, shallow depth of field, like a still from a Talos Principle-style sunlit world of pale stone ruins, cypress trees and sea. No text, no labels, no captions.

The eraser is COOL NEUTRAL GRAY rubber (clearly gray, like a slate-gray art eraser, NOT brown, NOT stone), worn crumbly edges, house-sized. Face: SLEEPY and GRUMPY, NOT angry: heavy droopy half-closed eyelids, dark tired bags under the eyes, a small sulky downturned pout. Small stubby rubber arms. Gray rubber crumbs fall off.

Three panels side by side: (1) CHASING: scrubbing forward across a sunlit stone causeway; behind it the world is rubbed out into blank white paper with gray crumbs flying; (2) LOOMING: low camera, the eraser rearing up enormous and leaning over us, face half in shadow, crumbs raining; (3) REDEEMED: the same eraser now classic friendly PINK rubber, eyes wide open, big happy smile, singing with mouth open, rosy cheeks, little colored-pencil sparkles, warm light.
