Concept-art sheet for an animated music video boss, "THE ERASER", as a cinematic stylised 3D render (soft matte rubber with subsurface scattering, Pixar-like appeal, golden-hour sun with long shadows like The Talos Principle, shallow depth of field). Clean pale paper-white background. No text, no labels.

THE ERASER: a HUGE classic rectangular rubber school eraser, as big as a house, made of COOL NEUTRAL GRAY rubber (not brown, not stone), worn crumbly edges, a cardboard paper sleeve band around its middle. Its face is SLEEPY and GRUMPY rather than angry: heavy droopy half-closed eyelids, tired bags under the eyes, a sulky wobbly pout; small stubby rubber arms. Gray rubber crumbs fall off it.

Three panels across one wide sheet: (1) CHASING: scrubbing along a sunlit world of floating stone ruins and cypress trees drawn in ink strokes, everything behind it rubbed out to blank white paper; (2) LOOMING: rearing up enormous, leaning over the camera, face in shadow; (3) REDEEMED: the same eraser now a classic friendly PINK eraser, eyes wide open, big happy smile, singing, rosy cheeks, colored-pencil sparkles.
