Painterly concept-art sheet for an animated music video boss, "THE ERASER", painted in rich gouache and colored pencil on cream paper with visible hatching and soft graphite construction lines, like a production art book page. No text, no labels.

THE ERASER: a HUGE classic rectangular rubber school eraser block, as big as a house, dull GRAY rubber with crumbly worn edges and a cardboard paper sleeve band around its middle, with a big SLEEPY-GRUMPY face: heavy droopy half-closed eyelids, bags under the eyes, sulky wobbly frown; small stubby rubber arms. Gray eraser crumbs and shavings constantly fall off it.

Three studies across the page: (1) CHASING: scrubbing forward through a hand-drawn world, leaving a blank white smear of erased paper and crumbs behind; (2) LOOMING: rearing up enormous, leaning over the viewer, face in shadow; (3) REDEEMED: the same eraser now a classic friendly PINK eraser, eyes open, big happy smile, singing, rosy cheeks, little rainbow pencil strokes around it.
