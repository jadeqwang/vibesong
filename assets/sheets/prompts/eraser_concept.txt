Concept-art sheet for an animated music video boss character, "THE ERASER". Cinematic stylised 3D render (soft subsurface rubber material, Pixar/Ghibli-meets-The-Talos-Principle lighting, golden-hour warm sun with long shadows, shallow depth of field), clean light paper-gray background, no text, no labels.

THE ERASER: a HUGE classic rectangular rubber school eraser block, as big as a house, dull GRAY rubber with crumbly worn edges and a cardboard paper sleeve band around its middle, with a big SLEEPY-GRUMPY face: heavy droopy half-closed eyelids, tired bags under the eyes, a sulky wobbly frown; small stubby rubber arms. Gray eraser crumbs and shavings constantly fall off it.

Show on one wide sheet, left to right:
1. CHASING: the eraser scrubbing along a sunlit ink-drawn world of floating stone platforms and cypress trees, everything behind it rubbed out to blank white paper, crumbs flying.
2. LOOMING: the eraser rearing up huge and leaning over the camera, face in shadow, eyes narrowed, crumbs raining down.
3. REDEEMED: the same eraser transformed into a classic friendly PINK eraser (warm bubblegum pink, soft glow, rosy cheeks), eyes wide open, big happy smile, singing with its mouth open, little colored-pencil sparkles around it.
