Colored-pencil "cutscene portrait" of Jade, the young woman of the attached character sheets, singing the line "my imagination is linked to my everyday life".
Composition: Jade in three-quarter view, head and shoulders, on the image-left/center third, looking slightly up and off to image-right with a bright, dreamy expression, mouth open singing. Around her on the cream paper, sketched in the same colored pencil: small everyday objects (a coffee mug, a notebook, a bicycle, a laptop, a potted plant, a bus ticket, house keys) floating at different sizes, all joined to each other and to a little thought-sparkle at her temple by a chain of BLUE chain links, like a hyperlink drawn as a real metal-and-blue chain, curving across the frame. Soft warm paper light. Background: mostly empty cream paper with light pastel hatching.
